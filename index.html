SECURITY CHECKLIST

Your safety starts with awareness.

SpendSmart never needs your PIN, CVV or full card number.

Demo safety tips

Protect your credentials

Never share your OTP, PIN, CVV or password — even with someone claiming to be from your bank.

Watch for fake links

Avoid unknown links in SMS, email and social media. Visit your bank's official app or website directly.

Enable transaction alerts

Turn on instant transaction notifications and review your statements regularly.

Use secure networks

Avoid making financial transactions over public Wi-Fi. Keep your devices and apps updated.

Report a suspicious transaction

Create a demo report record. This does not contact your bank.

For an actual unauthorized transaction, immediately contact your card issuer through its official channel and follow applicable bank and regulatory reporting guidance. Liability and timelines depend on your bank and local rules.
